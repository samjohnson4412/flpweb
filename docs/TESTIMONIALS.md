## Handling note — the "Grinch"

The Aug 2024 announcement and one testimonial both attribute the show's end to
"a grinch who lives nearby." Recommend **not** carrying that framing onto the
website. A neighbor dispute aired on a 501(c)(3)'s site invites scrutiny of the
organization, not the neighbor, and it dates badly.

The business plan already gives a cleaner and equally true account: the show
outgrew a residential property — 500+ visitors some nights — and the team chose
to evolve the mission rather than chase a bigger venue. That version is forward-
looking and makes the pivot sound like a decision instead of an eviction.

## Permissions

**OPEN:** Google Reviews and Facebook comments are public, but reusing a named
person's words as a marketing testimonial is a different act from them posting
it. Recommend asking the three relief testimonial authors for explicit
permission, or using first name + last initial.
