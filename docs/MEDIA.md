## Caveat

All of this coverage is of the **light show era**, under the Sylvan Ramble Lights
name. There is no press yet for the disaster-relief work. Framing matters: a
press page that is entirely pre-2024 could imply the organization peaked before
the pivot. Group by era, or lead with an "As seen on" logo strip and let the
detail sit lower.
