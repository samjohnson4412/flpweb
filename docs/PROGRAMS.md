# Programs & services

Source: *Business Plan for Florida Lantern Project (Formerly Sylvan Ramble Lights)*.

The plan organizes services on a **Before / During / After** disaster timeline.
That framing is the strongest thing in the document and should drive the site's
information architecture — see [SITE-MAP.md](SITE-MAP.md).

Almost none of this is on the live site today. The site lists four things in a
single sentence; the plan describes thirteen distinct services.

## Before disaster

| Service | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Disaster Preparedness Workshops | Training families and communities on emergency planning |
| Weather Station Installations | Weather stations and webcams feeding open-source weather networks, improving hyper-local forecasting |

## During disaster

| Service | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Evacuation & Transportation Assistance | Helping families evacuate in the hours before a disaster strikes |
| Real-Time Weather Updates | Forecasts, severe weather alerts, storm tracking |
| Disaster Support Hotline | Non-emergency hotline during storms — weather updates, home safety questions, next-step guidance |

## After disaster

| Service | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Wellness & Safety Checks | Locating and checking on family members, especially elderly or vulnerable people who are unreachable |
| Emergency Repairs | Immediate basic critical repairs to make homes safe |
| Home Cleanup & Debris Removal | Volunteer mobilization to help families clear damage |
| Roof & Property Inspections | Aerial/drone surveys producing inspection reports for insurance claims |
| Family Stability Support | Financial aid, childcare, essential resources; care packages, books, toys for children |
| Aid Navigation | Access to FEMA, SBA, and local grant programs |

## Revenue streams

**Contributed**
- Corporate & local business sponsorships
- Individual donations (one-time + monthly donor program)
- Grants & government funding (FEMA, local disaster recovery funds, private foundations)
- **Beacons of Hope** — annual gala, explicitly "nodding back to our light show roots"
- **Lanterns of Hope** — holiday fundraiser where sponsored lanterns symbolize hope

**Earned** (free for affected low-income families, sliding scale otherwise)
- Drone inspections & damage assessments
- Weather station installations & data services
- Emergency readiness kits
- **Lantern Network Membership** — paid membership with exclusive access to services
  including post-storm inspections

## Discrepancies to resolve

1. **Sandra Jimenez is Treasurer**, not generic "Board." A 501(c)(3) that shows
   no treasurer publicly looks less governed than it is. Easy credibility win.
2. **Legal name change** was planned for Fall 2025 ("DBA until official legal
   name change"). The footer still reads `Sylvan Ramble Lights, Inc.`
   **OPEN: did it complete?** Affects the footer, the Ch. 496 registration, and
   donation receipts.
3. **"Beacons of Hope" vs "Beacon of Hope Gala"** — the plan uses both. Pick one.
4. **Light show revival already has a name**: "Lanterns of Hope," the holiday
   fundraiser in the plan. It is nowhere on the site.
