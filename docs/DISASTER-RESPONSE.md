# Florida disaster response — Helene & Milton, 2024

Reconstructed from the organization's Facebook posts. **None of this appears on
the live website.** It is the most important missing content.

## Timeline

### Sept 27, 2024 — Hurricane Helene
> **URGENT** We are stepping outside of our normal mission to provide urgent
> support for those impacted by Hurricane Helene. IF YOU ARE IN NEED OF
> ASSISTANCE or would like to donate to those in need, visit our website.
>
> 100% of the funds raised will go directly to those in need, ensuring they
> receive immediate help during this challenging time. We will also provide
> regular updates on how your donations are being used through our social media
> channels.

Still operating as sylvanramblelights.com at this point. This is the moment the
organization pivots — worth marking as such in the story.

### Oct 6, 2024 — Hurricane Milton: evacuation lodging
> Evacuating from Hurricane Milton? We've secured discounted Hilton hotel rooms
> for $68/night in Orlando, with free breakfast and parking included.

**76 comments, 700 shares** — by far the highest-reach post. Concrete, specific,
immediately actionable. A model for how the site should write about services.

### Oct 8, 2024 — Milton: connectivity
> Need a phone plan to stay connected to friends and family during the storm?
> Unlimited text and call phone plans available free for 1 month. Must have an
> eSIM compatible phone.

This service appears nowhere in the business plan or on the site. It is a
genuinely unusual offering and should be captured.

### Oct 11, 2024 — Milton: free inspections
> **Free Roof and Home Inspections** — If your property has been impacted by
> Hurricane Milton, we are offering free roof and home inspections to help
> assess the damage and provide guidance on next steps. To request an
> inspection, fill out the form on our website or PM us. If you are experiencing
> an emergency, CALL 911.

Scoped to the Tampa Bay Area.

## Services actually delivered during Helene/Milton

1. Emergency hotel lodging (Hilton partnership, $68/night, Orlando)
2. Free eSIM phone plans, 1 month unlimited talk/text
3. Free roof and home inspections (Tampa Bay Area)
4. Transportation / evacuation assistance
5. Cleanup support
6. Direct financial aid — 100% pass-through

Aggregate per the business plan: **50+ individuals and families**, delivered with
**no budget**, through partnerships with hotels and community partners.

## Notes for the rebuild

- The Hilton and phone-plan offers are the single best proof that "Breaking
  Barriers" and "Innovative Solutions" are real values and not boilerplate.
  Specificity is the whole argument.
- Each response needs a permanent page, not just a Facebook post. Facebook posts
  are unsearchable, unlinkable in grant applications, and disappear from feeds.
- The graphics in these posts contain typos ("Messege us to book", "avalible").
  Worth fixing anything that gets reused on the site.
- "PM us to request one" doesn't scale and isn't accessible. The site should own
  the intake form so requests are tracked, not buried in a Messenger inbox.
